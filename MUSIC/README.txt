soundtrack seamlessly during gameplay, boss fights, and in the menus!

ABOUT THE ORIGINAL PC SOUNDTRACK
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

On the original 1997 PC CD-ROM, the music was stored as standard Red Book
CD-Digital Audio (CD-DA) tracks (Tracks 2 through 15 on the disc), rather than
files in the filesystem.

You can rip these tracks directly from your audio CD into MP3 format using any
standard CD ripper (e.g. Exact Audio Copy, Windows Media Player, iTunes,
cdparanoia, etc.).

Once copied into this "MUSIC/" folder, audio playback will start automatically
when entering levels or menus. You can adjust music and SFX volumes in-game
via the Options menu or Pause screen!
